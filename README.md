# Hi, I'm Agustín Acuña Pintos

**Earth Observation Scientist** · MSc Geomatics Engineering, Polytech Lille

Earth Observation Scientist from Uruguay, based in France since 2022. Finishing an MSc in Geomatics Engineering and Urban Planning at Polytech Lille, applying computer vision and deep learning to Earth Observation: burn scar segmentation, change detection, and automated feature extraction from satellite imagery.

Currently fine-tuning **Prithvi-100M** (IBM/NASA) on Sentinel-2 L2A to segment wildfire burn scars, and developing GeoAI pipelines for urban heat island analysis using multitemporal Landsat data.

## Featured Project

**[Wildfire Burn Scar Detection](https://github.com/aacunapintos/wildfire-burn-scar-detection)**

Semantic segmentation of wildfire burn scars on Sentinel-2 L2A imagery, fine-tuned from Prithvi-100M on the 2022 Corrientes fires in Argentina. Includes zero-shot generalization to unseen regions and T=2 temporal fusion.
